Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Your music

D-Jay Noodles analyses, separates and mixes your music on your own Mac. Your songs, and everything it learns about them, stay on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

What the app downloads

On first launch the app downloads the AI models it uses (about 1 GB) from public model hosts. It may also check for app updates. These requests don't include anything about you or your music.

Buying

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the seller of record. They collect the details needed to take payment and issue your licence; see their privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details so we can support your purchase.

No tracking

The app has no analytics or advertising trackers. This website uses no cookies.
