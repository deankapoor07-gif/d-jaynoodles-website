Questions

How long until my first set plays?

The first set on a new Mac takes a few minutes while D-Jay Noodles learns your songs and separates vocals from beats. It starts playing as soon as enough is ready. Every song it prepares is kept, so after that sets start in seconds.

Does it work with Spotify or Apple Music streaming?

No. It needs music files on your Mac or a drive: songs you've bought, downloaded or ripped. Streaming services don't let other apps use their songs.

Does it work with songs in other languages?

Yes. Tempo, key, beats and vocal separation all come from the sound, so music in any language mixes the same. The app itself is in English.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your music is analysed and mixed on your Mac. The only downloads are the AI models on first launch, and app updates.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The trial runs for 7 days with everything working. Buy a licence any time to keep it going.

Intel Mac?

Not at the moment. The vocal separation needs Apple Silicon to run at a usable speed.
